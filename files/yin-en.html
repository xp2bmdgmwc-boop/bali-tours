opening.

Each day is a step from the surface to the depth. From tension to stillness. From the role you play to who you truly are.

Day One
Cleansing
Attunement to the island

First contact with the energy of Bali. Pura Gunung Kawi Sebatu — a purification ceremony with a local monk. Night overlooking the volcano.

  • Purification ritual in sacred waters
  • Setting the intention of the journey
  • Night at Kintamani volcano
Day Two
Opening
The yin energy of water

NungNung Waterfall — the waterfall that opens the heart. Pura Ulun Danu Bratan water temple with feminine yin energies. Sunset at the ocean.

  • Meditation at the waterfall
  • Yin practice at the water temple
  • Dinner on the coast
Day Three
Power
Meeting the Guardian

Pura Agung Besakih — the place where questions are answered. Meeting the Shaman and the sacred Keris daggers. Ecstatic Dance.

  • Ecstatic Dance practice
  • Meeting the Keris master

Source

  • Evening gratitude ritual

Fixation

Photographic capture of the new state. The Alchemical Portrait is an encounter with your true self, recorded by a master.
